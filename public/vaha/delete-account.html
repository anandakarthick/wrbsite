Delete your Vaha AI account

Vaha AI — an AI tutoring app by KA Software

You can permanently delete your Vaha AI account and its associated data at any time. There are two ways to request deletion:

Option 1 — Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open the Vaha AI app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm on the “Delete account?” dialog.

Your account is deactivated immediately and removed from the service.

Option 2 — Request by email

Email support@vahaai.com from your registered address (or with your registered mobile number) and ask us to delete your account. We verify the request and complete the deletion, and confirm by reply.

What data is deleted and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Profile (name, mobile number, class, language, parent contact)Deleted / anonymised
Uploaded homework, photos, voice notes, documentsDeleted
Lessons, questions, doubts, tests, progress and scoresDeleted
Device push tokenDeleted
Payment/transaction records (order id, amount, date)Retained where required by law/accounting, then removed. Card details are never stored by us — payments are processed by Razorpay.

Records that must be kept for legal, tax, security or fraud-prevention reasons are retained only for the period the law requires (typically up to 8 years for financial records) and are then deleted or anonymised. Everything else is removed promptly.
