About

What is KA CRM?

KA CRM brings together contacts, deals, tasks, and conversations in one clean workspace. Its AI engine scores every lead based on behaviour and history, predicts which deals are likely to close, and automates follow-up reminders so nothing slips through. Built-in WhatsApp and email integration keeps all customer communication in one timeline, while customisable pipelines fit any sales process from real estate to SaaS.
