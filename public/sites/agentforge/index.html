About

What is AgentForge?

AgentForge is a visual platform for building AI agents that go beyond chat. Drag together triggers, AI reasoning steps, and actions to create agents that read incoming emails and draft replies, reconcile data between systems, generate weekly reports, monitor dashboards and escalate anomalies, or process approvals end-to-end. Agents connect to Gmail, WhatsApp, Slack, databases, and any REST API, run on schedules or events, and keep a full audit log of every action for compliance.

FAQ

Common Questions

How is this different from a chatbot?

Chatbots talk; agents act. AgentForge agents log into systems, move data, and complete multi-step workflows - with every action logged.

What stops an agent from doing something wrong?

Per-tool permissions, sandbox testing, human approval checkpoints for sensitive steps, and full audit trails. You define exactly what an agent may touch.

Do we need AI engineers to use it?

No - the visual builder is designed for operations teams. Our engineers help design your first agents during onboarding.
