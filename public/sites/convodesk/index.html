About

What is ConvoDesk?

ConvoDesk lets any business deploy a customer-support AI without writing code. Upload your product catalogue, FAQs, and policies, and the bot instantly answers customer questions in natural language across WhatsApp, web chat, and Instagram DMs. It books appointments, tracks orders, collects leads, and hands over to a human agent with full context when the conversation needs one. Supports English, Tamil, and Hindi out of the box, with a live dashboard showing every conversation and resolution rate.
