About

What is KartPOS?

KartPOS turns any tablet or desktop into a powerful billing counter. It syncs stock in real time between your shop floor and your ShopNest online store, supports barcode scanning and thermal printing, manages suppliers and purchase orders, and produces GST-ready reports at the end of every day. Works offline and syncs automatically when the connection returns — built for supermarkets, pharmacies, and retail chains.

FAQ

Common Questions

Does it work with my existing printer and scanner?

Yes - any standard barcode scanner and ESC/POS thermal printer works out of the box.

What happens when the internet goes down?

Nothing changes at the counter. KartPOS runs fully offline and syncs bills and stock automatically when you are back online.

Can it handle batch and expiry for a pharmacy?

Yes - batch-wise stock, expiry alerts, and scheduled-drug registers are supported.
